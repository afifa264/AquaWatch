OUR PURPOSE

Water is a shared resource.
Care should be, too.

AquaWatch is a community reporting concept designed to make local water concerns easier to document, discover, and follow up.

Help your community ↗
◉
THE AQUAWATCH APPROACH

From awareness to action.

⌁

Make issues visible

Give community members a simple way to report leaks, pressure problems, water logging, and quality concerns.

◉

Organise observations

Use categories, priorities, and status labels to help people understand what has been reported.

↗

Encourage follow-up

Keep the conversation transparent while reminding users that reports need appropriate real-world verification.

ⓘ

Important limitation: AquaWatch is an academic demonstration. It does not test water, contact authorities automatically, or verify submitted reports. Do not use the demo as an emergency reporting service.
